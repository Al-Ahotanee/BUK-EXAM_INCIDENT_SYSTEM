# BUK EIMS Branding Guide

## Brand position

BUK EIMS is the Bayero University Kano Exam Incident Management System. The interface should feel **institutional, trustworthy, calm, and accountable** rather than flashy. The design uses a strong blue foundation for institutional authority, a gold accent for emphasis and official action, and warm neutral surfaces for readability.

## Palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| BUK Blue | `#0B5C8E` | Primary buttons, headings, active navigation, links |
| BUK Navy | `#06385B` | Sidebar gradients, hero sections, dark backgrounds |
| BUK Sky | `#0875B4` | Secondary states and hover treatment |
| BUK Gold | `#C6A15B` | Accent borders, active indicators, highlights, key metrics |
| BUK Gold Light | `#E4C777` | Accent text on dark backgrounds |
| BUK Ink | `#071A2B` | High-emphasis dark surfaces and text |
| Warm Surface | `#F8FAFC` | Page backgrounds and neutral panels |

## Typography

Use **Inter** for body text, controls, tables, and labels. Use **Manrope** for display headings, product lockups, hero titles, and dashboard headings. Keep headings short, confident, and sentence case unless a workflow label is intentionally uppercase.

## Logo usage

The shared crest is stored at `assets/buk-logo.png`. Use a white or light neutral background behind the crest when it appears in compact navigation lockups. Do not stretch the image, recolor it, or place it on a visually noisy background. Keep the BUK EIMS wordmark aligned vertically with the crest.

## UI aesthetics

Use generous spacing, medium-radius cards, restrained shadows, thin neutral borders, and high-contrast primary actions. Use gold sparingly so that it signals official emphasis instead of becoming a dominant background color. Status badges should remain semantically colored: green for resolved, amber for investigation or hearing, blue for review, and neutral gray for pending or dismissed states.

## Product naming

Use **BUK EIMS** in navigation, page titles, deployment documentation, and user-facing product references. Use **Bayero University Kano** when identifying the institution in formal copy. Avoid legacy references to FUD, OIRMF, Federal University Dutse, or `fud.edu.ng`.
